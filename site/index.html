Plan your week, keep your notes in one place, and protect your focus time.

Nerdy is a free, open-source study planner for Windows, for students, teachers and anyone learning on their own.

Download for Windows How to install

Free beta for Windows 10 (version 2004 or later) and Windows 11 on Intel or AMD PCs. About 110 MB. Nerdy keeps itself up to date.

Plan your week

A calendar for classes, events and study blocks, with repeats and reminders before they start.

Study blocks that start themselves

Mark a block to start a focus session automatically. You get a one-minute heads-up and can start now or skip.

To-dos that don't slip

Reminders with due dates that stay on screen until you mark them done or snooze them.

Notes and topics

Rich notes and checklists, and topics that track your progress through each subject and what's due next.

Focus and Ultra-Focus

During a session Nerdy hides or closes the apps you didn't allow. Ultra-Focus goes full screen and is hard to leave, with an emergency exit that always works.

Classes

Teachers create a class with a join code and assign tasks. Students choose whether to share their focus progress.

Install Nerdy

  1. Download NerdyApp-win-Setup.exe and open it. It installs just for you, with no administrator rights needed, and puts Nerdy on your desktop and in the Start menu.
  2. While Nerdy is in beta, its installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC". Click More info, then Run anyway. You only see this when installing: updates install on their own.
  3. Sign in or create an account, and you're ready.

To uninstall, open Settings → Apps → Installed apps, find Nerdy and choose Uninstall. Your planner stays in your account, and a copy stays on your PC in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Nerdy until you delete that folder. All releases and their changes are on GitHub.

Your data

Sign in with email, Microsoft or Google to keep your planner in sync across your PCs. Nerdy doesn't show ads and doesn't sell your data. Because it's open source, anyone can check exactly what the app does. Read the privacy policy for the details.
